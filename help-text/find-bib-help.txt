find-bib — filter the canonical bibliography catalogue by fields

Usage:
  find-bib [filters...] [--cat]

Filters (repeatable; case-insensitive substrings):
  --author TXT
  --year YYYY
  --doi TXT
  --title TXT
  --abstract TXT

Matching:
  - AND across different fields
  - OR across repeated values of the same field

Catalogue:
  - searches the full ReferenceCatalog union (primary + secondary-only records)
  - primary metadata wins when a citation key occurs in both sources
  - BIB_JSON is the required primary source; ZOTERO_JSON is optional secondary data

Output:
  - default: print matching citation keys, one per line
  - --cat: print each match as BibTeX via cite2bib

Exit codes:
  - 0 at least one match emitted
  - 1 no matches, or a matched key could not be emitted by --cat
  - 2 usage/configuration error

Examples (list → then read one):
  find-bib --author vesper --year 2013 --title jump > keys.txt
  key=$(sed -n '1p' keys.txt)
  cite2bib "$key"
  # Or: cite2md --cat "$key"

Notes:
  - Secondary-only catalogue records may not have local BibTeX/fulltext artifacts.
  - Pair with cite2md or cite2bib after selecting a key.
